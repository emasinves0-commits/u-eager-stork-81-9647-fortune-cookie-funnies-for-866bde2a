Entry for the [Fortune Cookie Funnies for Engineers](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-fortune-cookie-funnies-for-866bde2a) bounty on make.fun: the funniest one-line fortune cookie message for a software engineer, 120 characters or fewer.

## The entry

> You will blame the AI for the outage until the postmortem names your commit.

76 characters. A fortune-cookie prophecy with a self-own reversal: you set up the AI as the scapegoat, then the postmortem indicts you — the funniest word, your commit, sits at the end.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (no trailing newline), after validating it is 1–120 characters. See [AGENT.md](AGENT.md) for how the line was chosen.
